Montagist is an AI video studio that lets one person make documentary-grade explainer videos: sourced research, real footage instead of slides, and your call at every gate — with your footage and finished videos kept on your own Mac.

# Montagist Agent Skill

This repository contains the Montagist Skill for Claude Code and Codex. Install the [Montagist Mac app](https://montagist.haoliucha.com/download), open it, and sign in before using the Skill to inspect or advance an episode through the local command-line tool.

Install the Skill:

```sh
npx skills add haoliucha/montagist -g -a claude-code codex -y
```

Tell the agent what you want to make or review. Only your spoken or written decision may pass or send back a gate, and the agent must preserve your exact words. Spending money, deleting an episode, and exporting publication materials prompt you again on your Mac. Your footage and finished videos stay on your Mac.
